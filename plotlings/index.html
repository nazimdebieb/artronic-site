Plotlings

A Shikaku puzzle in a vegetable garden. Each seed shows a number: draw its plot as a rectangle of exactly that many cells. Cover the whole garden, one seed per plot, and the radishes, carrots and pumpkins grow.

▶ Get it on Google Play

Features

  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and you can always find it by logic: no guessing
  • Hints that explain: the watering can shows the next certain plot, and why
  • 150 levels in 10 gardens to decorate, with rocks, mystery seeds, fences and square seeds, then an endless garden
  • A new grid every day, with a streak and a calendar
  • Weekly league of 50 players: every grid you solve earns dewdrops, climb from Bronze to Diamond
  • Your profile: a name and 63 collectible figurines, unlocked league after league
  • A free garden from 5×5 to 9×9 in three difficulties, 15 packs of 50 grids and a 3-minute rush
  • No lives, no energy, no subscription
  • Colour-blind patterns, larger numbers, plays offline
  • English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch

LumiGlade

Light up a night glade. Each wisp lights its row and its column until a rock or a mushroom stops the glow. Light every cell, never let two wisps see each other, and give each numbered mushroom exactly that many wisps next to it.

Coming soon to Google Play.

Features

  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and you can always find it by logic: no guessing
  • Hints that explain: each one shows the next certain wisp, and why
  • A new grid every day, with a streak and an archive of past days
  • Weekly league of 50 players: every grid you solve earns points, climb from Bronze to Diamond
  • Your profile: a name and 63 collectible figurines, unlocked league after league
  • Hundreds of levels, 15 packs of 50 grids, unlimited play and a 3-minute run
  • No lives, no energy, no subscription
  • Night and dawn themes, colorblind-friendly colors, big numbers, plays offline
  • English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch

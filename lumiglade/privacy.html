Privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

LumiGlade (“the game”) is a logic puzzle game for Android published by ND Inovation Groupe (Paris, France) under its Artronic brand (“Artronic”, “we”). This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

In short

Your progress stays on your device. Artronic collects no personal data such as your name, email or location, and has no server. The game shows no ads. It can measure anonymous usage statistics, but only if you accept when it asks. Optional in-game purchases, when they are offered, are handled entirely by Google Play.

Data stored on your device

To remember your game, LumiGlade saves the following on your device only:

  • the grids you solved, your times and stars, your daily streak, your achievements and your collection;
  • the grid you are playing, so you can resume it;
  • your profile (the player name you choose or the one drawn at random, and your figurine) and your weekly league (points, rank and past weeks). The other players of the league are simulated by the game on your device: your name and points are never sent anywhere;
  • your spark gems, your hints and what you bought, if anything;
  • your settings (language, theme, colorblind colors, big numbers, sound, vibration, daily reminder, statistics choice).

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Erase my progress in the game, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the game.

Data we collect

Artronic has no account system and no server of its own. The game does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files, and it does not use the advertising ID. Artronic itself collects nothing. The only service that can receive data is the optional usage statistics below, which are off until you accept.

Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

After your first finished grid, the game asks whether we may measure anonymous usage statistics. If you say yes, the game uses Google Firebase Analytics to receive events such as: a grid started, finished or left, its size and difficulty, the time taken, hints asked for and shown, a daily grid started or finished and the length of your streak, a setting changed, a result shared, and basic technical information (app version, Android version, device model, country and language). No name, email or account is collected, and the statistics do not use the advertising ID. The purpose is to see which grids are too hard and to improve the game. The data is processed by Google (see Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's privacy information) and is kept in a project owned by Artronic. You can change your choice at any time with Settings → Anonymous statistics; turning it off stops the collection immediately. If you say no, nothing is sent.

Permissions

  • Vibration: short vibrations as you place your wisps. You can turn them off in the game's settings.
  • Notifications and alarms (optional): if you turn on the daily reminder, the game schedules a notification on your device at the time you choose. It is created and shown by your phone: no server is involved. You can turn it off in the game or in your phone's settings.
  • Internet and network state: only to send the statistics if you accepted them, and for Google Play purchases; the game itself plays fully offline.
  • Billing: lets Google Play handle the optional purchases described below.

Sharing your result

The Share button only opens your phone's share sheet with a short message you can edit; it never shows the solution. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Purchases

The Shop lists optional purchases: “No Ads” (one-time; the game shows no ads today, and it also gives hints beyond the free ones without a video if videos are added later), “Big packs” (one-time, unlocks the largest puzzle packs) and packs of 10, 30 or 100 hints (consumable: hints are added to a stock kept on your device and used up as you play). Until they are switched on, the Shop shows them as “Soon” and nothing can be bought. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play: Artronic never sees your payment details, name or card. The game only learns from Google Play whether a purchase was made, so it can unlock the content or add the hints, and it stores the result on your device. “Restore purchases” in the Shop or the settings asks Google Play again after a reinstall or on a new phone; used hints cannot be restored. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

LumiGlade shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. If ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated first, on this page.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Artronic does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

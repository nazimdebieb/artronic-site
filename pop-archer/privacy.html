Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Pop Archer (“the game”) is an arcade game for Android published by ND Inovation Groupe (Paris, France) under its Artronic brand (“Artronic”, “we”). This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

In short

Your progress stays on your device. Artronic collects no personal data such as your name, email or location. The only data the game sends to a server is what the optional daily world leaderboard needs (see below), and you can switch it off. The game also shows ads from Google AdMob and sells optional items through Google Play, and these Google services process some data as described below.

Data stored on your device

To remember your game, Pop Archer saves the following on your device only:

  • levels completed, stars and Survival scores;
  • coins, unlocked outfits, arrow effects, weapons and boosts;
  • your settings (sound effects, music, vibration, controls, language);
  • the dates of your daily gift and free chests, and whether you have seen the tutorial.

Apart from the leaderboard entry described below, this data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the game, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the game.

Daily world leaderboard

Pop Archer has a daily world leaderboard. Unless you turn it off, the game sends one entry per day to a database hosted by Google (Google Cloud Firestore, in Europe), made of:

  • a player name generated by the game (2 to 16 characters; it is not your real name);
  • the outfit you wear, your player level and your points for the day;
  • the time of the last update;
  • a random anonymous identifier created by Firebase Authentication (anonymous sign-in), which is not linked to your Google account, your email or your name.

Entries are visible to other players in the leaderboard. Google processes technical data such as your IP address when your device connects to these services. Entries are only used to rank players and are deleted automatically by the game after a few days. Leaderboard rivals shown next to real players are clearly labeled and are not real people.

You stay in control: switch off Show me in the world ranking in the game to stop sending data, and Settings → Reset progress removes your entry of the day from the leaderboard first. You can also write to us (see Contact) and we will delete your entry. See Firebase privacy information.

Data we collect

Apart from the leaderboard entry above, none. Artronic has no account system and no analytics, and does not receive your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files; the game does not access them.

Permissions

  • Vibration: short vibrations when you pop bubbles or get hit. You can turn them off in the game's settings.
  • Internet and network state: to load ads, to talk to Google Play for purchases and to use the world leaderboard. The game itself plays offline.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by Google AdMob, see below. You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).
  • Billing: lets Google Play handle in-app purchases.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads never play during gameplay. You can choose to watch a short video ad for a reward (for example to continue or to double your coins), and a short ad may sometimes show when you leave a results screen. Buying “No ads” removes the ads you don't choose.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time with Settings → Privacy choices. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Optional items (coin packs, the season pass, “No ads”, the armory and starter packs, the piggy bank) are sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google: Artronic never sees your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation of the purchase, used on your device to give you the item and, for one-time items, to restore it if you reinstall the game. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Delete your data

You can ask us to delete the data we hold about you at any time. The only data we hold is your daily world leaderboard entry (generated player name, outfit, player level, points of the day and an anonymous identifier). To delete it:

  • In the game: open Settings → Reset progress. Your entry of the day is removed from the leaderboard first. Switching off Show me in the world ranking also stops any new data from being sent.
  • By email: write to artronic.n@gmail.com with the subject “Pop Archer data deletion” and your player name as shown in the leaderboard. We will delete your entry and reply to confirm.
  • Automatically: old leaderboard entries are deleted by the game after a few days.

Progress saved on your device is not sent to us: you can delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game. Data held by Google (ads, purchases) is managed through your Google account.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Artronic does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

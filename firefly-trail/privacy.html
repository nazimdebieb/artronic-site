Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Firefly Trail (“the game”) is a logic puzzle game for Android published by ND Inovation Groupe (Paris, France) under its Artronic brand (“Artronic”, “we”). This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

In short

Your progress stays on your device. Artronic collects no personal data and has no server. The current version of the game shows no ads and collects no statistics. Two optional one-time purchases exist, handled entirely by Google Play.

Data stored on your device

To remember your game, Firefly Trail saves the following on your device only:

  • the grids you solved, your times and stars, your daily streak and your achievements;
  • the grid you are playing, so you can resume it;
  • your settings (language, theme, colorblind colors, sound effects, vibration, daily reminder).

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Erase my progress in the game, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the game.

Data we collect

None. Artronic has no account system and no server, and receives none of your data. The game does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Permissions

  • Vibration: short vibrations as you draw your trail. You can turn them off in the game's settings.
  • Notifications (optional): if you turn on the daily reminder, the game schedules a notification on your device at the time you choose. It is created and shown by your phone: no server is involved. You can turn it off in the game or in your phone's settings.
  • Internet and network state: required by the app platform; the game itself plays fully offline.
  • Billing: lets Google Play handle the two optional purchases, “Remove ads” and “Premium packs” (the 7×7, 8×8 and 9×9 puzzle packs).

Sharing your result

The Share button only opens your phone's share sheet with a short message you can edit. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Purchases

Firefly Trail offers two optional one-time purchases: “Remove ads” and “Premium packs”. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play: Artronic never sees your payment details, name or card. The game only learns from Google Play whether a purchase was made, so it can unlock the content, and it stores that fact on your device. “Restore purchases” in the settings asks Google Play again after a reinstall or on a new phone. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Possible future changes (ads, statistics)

Later versions may add optional ads (Google AdMob) and anonymous usage statistics (only if you accept). Before any of these is turned on, this policy will be updated to describe the data involved (for example the advertising ID used by Google AdMob), and in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game will ask for your consent first.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Artronic does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact
